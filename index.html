A developer focused on creating innovative solutions that sit at the intersection of education and technology.

With a background spanning computer science and education, I've dedicated my learning to building tools that make knowledge more accessible, engaging, and personalized. My work focuses on leveraging modern technology to craft adaptive digital experiences for every learner.

What started as curiosity—tinkering with code and building simple websites—has grown into the central passion of my life. Over the last two years I've been on a self-directed journey, diving deep into software development with a clear goal: to build the next generation of educational tools.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring the latest in AI research, contributing to open-source, or mentoring aspiring developers. I believe technology can genuinely democratize education worldwide.
